Common questions

Where are my receipts stored? On your iPhone and in your private iCloud. Turn on iCloud for the app to sync between devices.

I bought the unlock on another device. Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase.

How do I move to a new phone? Sign in with the same Apple ID and keep iCloud on. Your receipts come back automatically.
